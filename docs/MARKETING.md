# Marketing System

Status: planning workspace and editable Markdown campaign. No posts, emails, ads or paid campaigns have been sent or launched.

## Positioning

An original creature-collecting adventure for curious players who enjoy cozy worlds and short discovery sessions. Treat this as a hypothesis until audience interviews validate it.

## Content loop

Approved game build → capture real footage → draft hook/caption → review claims and asset rights → schedule with explicit authorization → measure outcomes → record learning. Do not advertise a planned feature as available.

## Launch sequence

Concept diary, creature introductions, actual encounter demo, opt-in playtest, feedback-driven iteration, release announcement when a playable release exists. All screenshots must identify prototype visuals where relevant.

## Initial week

Monday: meet Mossling, a 15-second personality reveal.
Wednesday: show one complete encounter, with prototype label.
Friday: share the lantern-tree story concept.
Sunday: ask the audience which habitat feels most intriguing.

## Measurement

Maintain channel, asset ID, hypothesis, date, impressions, qualified visits, opt-in signups, demo completion and feedback themes. Use actual measurements only. A low response calls for revisiting the hook/audience, not fabricating social proof.

## Reusable brief

Audience; player benefit; proof from the current build; hook; visual sequence; caption; call to action; rights/provenance; reviewer; planned channel; success criterion. Budget and publication require a separate authorized campaign.
