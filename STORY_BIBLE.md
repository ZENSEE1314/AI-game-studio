# Echobound — Story Bible

Status: sample narrative, not a commissioned final story.

## World rule

Echoes embody small memories of places. They cannot freely rewrite history; they reveal fragments through actions and sounds. A Listener hears these fragments but must interpret them.

## Protagonist

The player is a new Listener with no fixed name, gender or backstory. Dialogue should support that openness.

## Characters

Mossling hides lost seeds in forgotten gardens. Emberfox guides travelers with warmth. Ripplefin stores melodies beneath quiet water. Avoid making species personality an absolute rule for every individual.

## Chapter 01: The Quiet Grove

Arrival: a lantern tree has stopped glowing. Discovery: a rustle reveals Mossling. Objective: recover a misplaced seed. Resolution: the tree returns a single note, hinting that other habitats have fallen silent. Reward: first companion, future grove access flag.

## Quest specification

ID `grove_lost_seed`; entry `intro_seen`; objective `seed_found`; completion `seed_returned`; reward `mossling_companion`; next `beacon_in_rain`. These flags describe planned quest logic; the current browser combat demo does not implement quests.

## Next chapters

A Spark in the Rain: Emberfox restores a village beacon. Beneath Still Water: Ripplefin uncovers a melody that belongs to no living Echo. The mystery expands through evidence rather than exposition dumps.

## Voice and continuity

Warm, curious, lightly mysterious. Use short readable dialogue. No claim that the player actually visited a location unless the system establishes it. Track discovered facts, character knowledge, quest prerequisites and reward uniqueness in each script review.
